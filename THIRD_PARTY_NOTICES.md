## Unicode emoji catalog

The local [catalog data](<src/emoji-data.json>) contains the full, fully-qualified
[Unicode Emoji 13.1](https://unicode.org/Public/emoji/13.1/emoji-test.txt) set.
English and Russian names/keywords come from
[CLDR 38.1](https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr/tree/367b79b4a86a9cb12314158897d950268dc77b02),
including annotationsDerived. The [catalog module](<src/emoji-catalog.js>) and generated
metadata identify immutable source URLs and SHA256 fingerprints. The explicit
[generator](<scripts/build-emoji-data.mjs>) reproduces the data; normal builds/tests
and the installed plugin do not fetch emoji resources. No emoji images or fonts are bundled.
